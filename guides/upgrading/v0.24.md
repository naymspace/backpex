# Upgrading to v0.24

## Bump Your Deps

Update Backpex to the latest version:

```elixir
defp deps do
  [
    {:backpex, "~> 0.24.0"}
  ]
end
```

## Unused pagination and order helpers removed from `Backpex.LiveResource`

The index view validates its pagination, order and search params with `Backpex.PaginationValidation`. These public
helpers of `Backpex.LiveResource` were no longer called by Backpex and are removed:

- `order_options_by_params/4`
- `parse_integer/3`
- `validate_page/2`
- `value_in_permitted_or_default/3`

`order_options_by_params/4` also converted the `order_by` and `order_direction` params with
`String.to_existing_atom/1`, which raises for a param that is not an existing atom.

If your app calls one of them, use `Backpex.PaginationValidation` instead: `build/2` validates `page`, `per_page`,
`order_by` and `order_direction` against your options (with `init_order` already resolved to a map) and falls back to defaults for invalid values, and `clamp_page/2`
replaces `validate_page/2`. `parse_integer/3` and `value_in_permitted_or_default/3` have no replacement; they are
small enough to copy into your app.
